## 🌅 What is this?

This is my **personal coding diary** — a day-by-day log of what I'm learning, breaking, fixing, and building. Think of it as a developer journal meets progress tracker meets 2 AM debugging confessional.

No filters. No polish. Just raw, honest growth. 🌱

---

## 📂 Structure

```
📓 daily-diary/
├── 📅 YYYY-MM-DD.md     ← Daily entries
├── 🗓️ weekly-reviews/   ← Weekly recaps & reflections
├── 💡 learnings/        ← Concepts I finally "got"
├── 🐛 bugs-hall-of-fame/ ← My most humbling moments
└── 🏆 wins/             ← Celebrating the small stuff
```

---

## ✍️ What a typical entry looks like

Each day has:

- **🎯 Goal of the day** — What I set out to do
- **⚡ What actually happened** — Real talk
- **🔍 What I learned** — The actual takeaway
- **🐞 Bugs squashed** — Proof that I suffer for my craft
- **📌 Tomorrow's plan** — Accountability in writing
